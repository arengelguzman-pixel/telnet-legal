Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Our application does not collect or store personal data from end users. It only handles authentication tokens for the official social media accounts owned by Telnet Systems S.R.L.

How to request deletion

If you believe we hold any data related to you, send an email to contacto@telnet.com.bo with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".

We will respond within 30 days and confirm deletion of any applicable records.

Revoking platform access

You can also revoke access to our application at any time via your platform settings:

  • Meta — Business Integrations
  • TikTok — App Permissions
  • X — Connected Apps
  • LinkedIn — Permitted Services
