Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Telnet Systems S.R.L. ("Telnet", "we", "us") handles information in connection with our social media publishing application.

1. Information We Process

The application accesses only:

  • Authentication tokens issued by Meta, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn for our own corporate accounts.
  • Content (images, video, captions) created by Telnet Systems for publishing to our own accounts.
  • Basic post metadata (post IDs, timestamps) returned by the platforms.

We do not collect personal data from end users, followers, commenters, or any third parties.

2. How We Use Information

Tokens and content are used solely to publish content to Telnet Systems' own official social media accounts.

3. Data Storage

Tokens are stored encrypted in our internal automation infrastructure (n8n on Railway). Access is restricted to authorized Telnet Systems personnel.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. Data is transmitted only to the official APIs of Meta, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn for the purpose of publishing.

5. Data Retention

Tokens are retained while valid. Published content remains on the respective platforms according to their own retention policies.

6. Data Deletion

To request data deletion, see our Data Deletion page.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy. The "Last updated" date will reflect changes.
